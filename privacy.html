Privacy Policy

When to Wear

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Information We Use

When to Wear uses your device's location, with your permission, to provide local weather information and outfit recommendations.

Weather Data

Your location coordinates are transmitted to Open-Meteo, a third-party weather service, to retrieve weather information. Open-Meteo may process and retain technical information, including IP addresses and location coordinates, according to its own privacy policy.

Open-Meteo's privacy policy is available at https://open-meteo.com/en/terms.

Locally Stored Preferences

Your temperature preferences and outfit feedback are stored locally on your device. We do not operate user accounts or an online database to store these preferences.

Advertising and Tracking

When to Wear does not display advertisements or use your information for cross-app advertising tracking.

Your Choices

You can disable location access at any time through your device settings. You can remove locally stored app data by deleting the app.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy as the app changes. Updates will be posted on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, please use the contact information on our support page.
